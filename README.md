# Agentic Research @ LSST DESC

This repository is a shared home for agentic research within the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC). It brings together scientists interested in exploring how AI agents can support research, exchanging practical experience, and developing tools and practices for transparent, reproducible science.

We welcome ideas, workflows, and community activities from across DESC. This space can support multiple initiatives and collaborations as members explore new scientific questions and ways of working.

[![Join the discussion](docs/assets/join-discussion.svg)](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions)

## Get involved

**Discuss research ideas.** Use [General](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/general) to explore scientific questions, find collaborators, and suggest activities. You do not need prior agentic AI experience or a fully designed solution to join the conversation.

**Join Agentic AI Office Hours.** These planned informal sessions are open to DESC members interested in agentic research. Bring questions about tools, workflows, evaluation, or research ideas. [Learn about office hours](docs/office-hours.md) and ask in [Office Hours & Questions](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/office-hours-questions). Session details are to be announced.

**Share what you learn.** Use [Show & Tell](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) for useful tools, demonstrations, experiences, and lessons from your research.

## Resources

**Run coding agents on ALCF-hosted models.** With a NERSC account, you can connect OpenCode, Pi, Claude Code, or Codex to open-weight models hosted by ALCF, with no usage caps. [Read the ALCF inference guide](docs/alcf-inference.md).

## 🚀 First activity: Lightcone Research Pilot


The **Lightcone Research Pilot** is a first activity within this broader effort. Participating DESC members will test and help improve **Lightcone Research's agentic tooling** on real LSST science questions and workflows. The [Lightcone Research team](https://lightconeresearch.org/) brings **focused engineering, compute, and model inference** to this work, with the aim of understanding where its tools are scientifically useful and developing best practices the Collaboration can adopt more broadly. Because resources are limited and the aim is a **focused effort with fast turnaround**, the Lightcone team plans to extend this dedicated support to a **limited number of projects**, subject to capacity.

The [call for ideas is now open](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/2), with proposals due by **16 October 2026**. We plan to initiate participating projects as sprints at **DESC Sprint Week**, where we will kick off selected projects.

[![Submit a project idea](docs/assets/submit-project-idea.svg)](https://github.com/LSSTDESC/agentic-research/discussions/new?category=lightcone-research-pilot-proposals)

➡️ **[Read more about the Lightcone Research Pilot](docs/lightcone-pilot/README.md).**

**What is Lightcone Research building**: Lightcone Research is developing tooling that helps you plan and run analyses with Claude Code or Codex on your own code and data, while keeping you in charge of the scientific choices. It records those choices and the code, inputs, and environment behind each result, making the work easier to inspect, reproduce, and share. You can explore the analysis in JupyterLab and build reports linked to its results. Explore the [Lightcone Research Stack documentation](https://docs.lightconeresearch.org/) or try the [worked cosmology example](https://docs.lightconeresearch.org/guides/first-analysis) to see it in action.
